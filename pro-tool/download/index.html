Getting set up

  1. Download the app.

    Windows, about 400 MB. It is one folder: unzip it anywhere and run PokemonChampionsTool.exe.

    Download for Windows

  2. Paste the key in.

    On first run the app is in the free tier. Click Upgrade in the sidebar, then I have a key, and paste the key above. The app restarts once and Pro is on.

  3. That is it.

    The key is checked once and then stored, so the app works offline from then on. It re-checks quietly in the background while your subscription runs.

Worth knowing

  • One key, your machines. There is no device limit and no account to make. Use it on your desktop and your laptop.
  • Cancelling is not a lockout. Stop the subscription and the app drops back to the free tier at the end of the period you paid for. Your teams and your box stay where they are.
  • Updates are automatic. The app checks for a new version when it starts. You will not need this page again.
