Why there is a paid plan at all

It pays for the data

A dedicated machine collects ladder snapshots for all 262 ranked Pokemon every day, in both formats, and the result is cleaned, checked and published. The website, the Team Builder, the Damage Calculator, the daily meta trends and the public API are free and stay free, with no advertising and nothing sold on.

Pro subscriptions are the only thing paying for that. Team Evaluation, Auto Build, Test against Tournament Teams and the Solver are the parts that cost real computing time, which is why they are the ones Pro unlocks.
