Prices in euro. Both Pro plans start with a 7-day free trial; cancel inside it and you are not charged.

Already subscribed? Enter the key from your email under Get Pro → I have a key in the Team Builder, and in the Companion app. Lost it? Send it again.

Want to cancel? Manage or cancel your subscription at any time through the Stripe Customer Portal. Pro then keeps working to the end of the period you are in — the free trial, or the month or year you have paid for — and for up to a week after that, while the licence is checked again. After that the website and the Companion app go back to Free; your teams and your Box stay exactly where they are.

The bonus

The Companion app comes with Pro

The Companion is a Windows app that runs beside the game. With Pro it reads the running game: it recognises the opponent's six from team preview, predicts their sets, and keeps lead and back suggestions current while you play. Your Box and teams follow you there through sync, and the same licence key unlocks it.

Why there is a paid plan at all

It pays for the data

A dedicated machine collects ladder snapshots for all 264 ranked Pokemon every day, in both formats, and the result is cleaned, checked and published. The website, the Team Builder, the Damage Calculator, the daily meta trends and the public API are free and stay free, with no advertising and nothing sold on.

Pro subscriptions are the only thing paying for that. Team Evaluation, Auto Build and Test against Tournament Teams are the parts that cost real computing time, which is why they are the ones Pro unlocks.
